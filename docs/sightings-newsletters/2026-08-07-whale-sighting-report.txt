Fred Horn

-

And just like that, they're back! K-Pod appeared on the west side of San Juan Island this morning, ending a nearly 7 week long drought with no Southern Residents in the inland waters of the Salish Sea (and just one day after I predicted they wouldn't visit us at all in August). This is K-Pod's first visit to the central Salish Sea since early March. They bombed their way up the west side of San Juan Island and continued north to the Strait of Georgia. We didn't catch up with them ourselves, but the Center for Whale Research got out with them and will have their encounter summary to share in due time. In case you missed them too, here's a short clip of them vocalizing on the Lime Kiln hydrophone hosted by The Whale Museum and SMRU, Link to audio clip.
